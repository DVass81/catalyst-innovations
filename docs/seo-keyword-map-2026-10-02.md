## Content and measurement rules

- Six Insights guides support the six broad audience groups with practical steps, illustrative examples and primary references; do not publish tool metrics as customer proof.
- Existing Elevate, HOA and Oxendine notes keep their supplied relationship and prototype statuses. Do not turn discussions or goals into endorsements or measured results.
- Track successful delivered inquiries separately from email draft creation, email clicks and booking-link clicks. Until delivery is configured, draft actions are not confirmed leads.
- Search Console baseline after verification: indexed pages, queries, impressions, clicks and average position. Analytics baseline after consent-enabled activation: visits, engaged actions and successful inquiries. Unavailable values remain unavailable, never zero.
- Repeat comparisons with the same date window and distinguish brand from non-brand searches. This document establishes a baseline, not an automatic monitoring schedule.
- No subscription upgrades, advertisements, paid article generation or paid keyword recalculations were used.
