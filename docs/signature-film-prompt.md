## Full resolved prompt

One complete 15-second, 16:9 silent motion-design film for Catalyst Innovations. Seven edited camera shots within one consistent software design world. Tell the story of one service request becoming a prepared quote, explicit approval, a scheduled job, linked materials, recorded completion and an invoice ready for review. The same illustrative record, JOB 104 — Equipment service, carries through every operational shot. The viewer should understand that Catalyst builds custom software connecting the work and information between business functions. These are illustrative screens, not recordings of a client product.

design world:
Warm cream studio background #FAF7F1, mostly opaque pearl-white software surfaces, deep navy #0A1628 headings and side rails, Catalyst steel blue #4A8FD4 along the connection path, darker blue #2F5D8F for readable accents. Rigid interface panels with modest thickness, small rounded corners, narrow silver highlights and clean blue-edged glass only at the outer edges. Soft directional shadows create real depth. Keep readable content opaque and uncluttered. One brief navy studio passage emphasizes the approval and scheduling handoff. Use restrained sans-serif typography and substantial whitespace. No third-party marks, people, phones, real customer information, numerical performance charts or decorative code. Do not imitate a client screenshot.

motion rules:
Use coherent three-dimensional geometry and real parallax. Move one piece of information through recognizable interfaces; do not replace the subject with unrelated dashboards. A narrow steel-blue line connects successive panels and carries one deliberate activation pulse after the corresponding action occurs. Camera moves should be approximately ten to fifteen degrees for arcs, with deliberate ease-out landings. No spinning, flying through text, bouncing, strobing or continuous orbiting. Objects retain their shape. Apply subtle blur during travel only. Text stays sharp, flat to the viewer and stationary during its reading hold. Keep the small “Illustrative workflow” label visible throughout. Operational shots retain “JOB 104” and “Equipment service”. Do not add other readable names or figures.
