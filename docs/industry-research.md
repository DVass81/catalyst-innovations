# Industry content research

Reviewed 2026-09-30. Public bullets in `data/industryNeeds.ts` are Catalyst's editorial synthesis of operational problems, not survey quotations or prevalence claims. Proposed software responses are our recommendations, not claims made by the sources. Software does not solve workforce shortages, guarantee compliance, or guarantee financial outcomes. Financial pages describe internal operations, not underwriting or financial advice.

| Industry | Primary source | Basis for editorial treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Manufacturing | [NAM manufacturing trends](https://nam.org/wp-content/uploads/securepdfs/2025/01/NAM-2025-Manufacturing-Trends.pdf) | Supply disruption, production and workforce pressures; connect job and material records. |
| Financial institutions | [FDIC community bank third-party guide](https://www.fdic.gov/news/financial-institution-letters/2024/third-party-risk-management-guide-community-banks) | Vendor oversight and retained institutional responsibilities; organized evidence and review workflows. |
| Credit unions | [NCUA supervisory priorities](https://ncua.gov/regulation-supervision/letters-credit-unions-other-guidance/ncuas-2025-supervisory-priorities) | Operational oversight, third parties and reporting; service handoffs are a proposed discovery topic. |
| Logistics | [ASCM visibility](https://www.ascm.org/ascm-insights/gaining-clarity-the-power-of-supply-chain-visibility/) | Shipment information, delays and coordination. |
| Construction | [AGC workforce survey](https://news.agc.org/workforce-development/workforce-shortages-delay-projects/) and [AGC outlook](https://www.agc.org/2025-construction-hiring-and-business-outlook) | Scheduling and cost pressure; change-control workflows are our proposed response to changing work. |
| Electrical contractors | [NECA labor units](https://www.necanet.org/education/publications/neca-manual-of-labor-units-%28mlu%29) | Estimating depends on labor, material handling and drawings; preserve revisions and job requirements. |
| Welding and fabrication | [AWS fabricator accreditation](https://www.aws.org/Businesses/Facility-Accreditation/CWF/) | Documented procedures and quality systems; digital records support human review, not certification. |
| Industrial services | [SMRP body of knowledge](https://smrp.org/learning-resources/smrp-library/body-of-knowledge/) | Planning, equipment reliability and work management. |
| Professional services | [AICPA workflow capacity](https://www.aicpa-cima.com/resources/article/beyond-email-using-ai-and-automation-to-enhance-capacity) | Representative accounting-service source on administrative workflows; other professions require discovery. |
| Field service | [PHCC environmental scan](https://www.phccweb.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/2025-PHCC-Environmental-Scan.pdf) | Representative service-trades source on scheduling, routes and cost pressures, not every field-service business. |
| Schools and athletics | [NFHS administrator responsibilities](https://nfhs.org/stories/documenting-what-an-athletic-administrator-does) and [NFHS challenges](https://www.nfhs.org/stories/greatest-challenges-facing-high-school-athletic-directors) | Event scheduling, communication and fundraising workload. |
| Churches and nonprofits | [National Council of Nonprofits workforce report](https://www.councilofnonprofits.org/reports/2023-nonprofit-workforce-survey-results) | Limited staff capacity; campaign and reporting workflows are proposed administrative applications, not church-specific survey findings. |
| Small business | [NFIB problems and priorities](https://www.nfib.com/small-business-problems-and-priorities/?tab=Problems+and+Priorities) | Cost and workload pressures; process ownership and integration recommendations are editorial. |
| Multi-location | [IFA operations consistency](https://www.franchise.org/2019/04/best-practices-promoting-consistency-within-a-system/) | Consistency, guidance and shared information; franchise evidence is representative of multi-location work. |
| Plumbing | [PHCC 2025 scan](https://www.phccweb.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/2025-PHCC-Environmental-Scan.pdf) | Material costs and routing; quote and completion handoffs are workflow recommendations. |
| HVAC | [PHCC 2026 scan overview](https://www.phccweb.org/news/phcc-releases-2026-industry-environmental-scan-highlighting-key-trends-and-opportunities/) | Service industry pressures and technology; seasonal scheduling, equipment histories and parts are proposed workflow areas. |
| Painting | [PCA standards](https://www.pcapainted.org/industry-standards/) and [PCA estimating](https://www.pcapainted.org/blog/10-tips-for-estimating/) | Scope misunderstandings, surface measurement and extra work; no legal interpretation of standards. |
| HOA | [CAI communication](https://hoaresources.caionline.org/how-to-avoid-communication-breakdown-across-your-associations/) and [maintenance practices](https://foundation.caionline.org/publications/best-practices/maintenance/) | Request ownership, communication and maintenance follow-through. |
| Warehousing | [MHI warehouse operations](https://www.mhi.org/blog/126152/ai-in-the-modern-warehouse) and [ASCM inventory curriculum](https://www.ascm.org/globalassets/documents--files/learning--development/cltd-2.0-final-ecm-preview.pdf) | Inventory accuracy, repeatable execution and ordering tradeoffs. |

No statistics, customer results, compliance promises or guaranteed savings are reproduced. Each page has three distinct paired bullets and retains its existing calculator recommendations and inquiry context.
