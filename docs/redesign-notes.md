## Original film brief, ready for the requested generation route

12-second silent 16:9 motion study for Catalyst Innovations. Warm cream background, navy and steel-blue vector objects, soft paper shadows. Fixed orthographic camera. No photorealistic business UI, no screenshots, no text, no client marks or numbers. A message card, document, calendar, materials box and invoice sheet form a clear left-to-right business process.
0–3 seconds: five disconnected objects in separate clusters, slightly tilted, with generous empty space.
3–9 seconds: each object moves gently into a horizontal row; a blue path connects message to quote, scheduling, materials and invoice in order. Each handoff has a deliberate ease-out settle. No cuts or camera spins.
9–12 seconds: hold the complete connected flow, all five objects clearly visible. No looping reset. No music, speech or sound effects. Captions and exact business labels are rendered by the website, not generated into the film.

## Calculator rules

24 calculators use shared field definitions and pure calculation functions. Reporting time and customer onboarding supplement the 22 specifically enumerated in the approved table. Examples are illustrative, never benchmarks or prices. Numerical inputs are not sent to analytics. Cross-tool sums are deliberately absent to avoid double counting.
