## Complete resolved film prompt

[Shot 1] Cinematic premium photoreal product-metaphor film, one continuous unbroken 20-second take, no editorial cuts or dissolves, landscape 16:9. This is a visual metaphor for custom business software connecting customers, jobs, materials and accounting, not an advertisement for physical computer hardware. All processes are illustrative. Stage an unbranded late-1980s beige desktop computer with chunky CRT screen, horizontal base unit and keyboard in a warm cream architectural studio. A few paper forms and folders sit nearby. Soft natural studio lighting, restrained cool rim highlights, realistic beige plastic, brushed metal, ivory process cards, navy #0A1628 and Catalyst blue #4A8FD4 accents. Preserve the recognizable computer and spatial continuity throughout. No people, numbers, words, logos, watermarks or simulated readable application screens. Audio completely silent: no narration, music or effects.

[0.00–3.00] Open on the complete old computer in a composed three-quarter view, full silhouette visible with generous frame margins. Small deliberate camera push. A subtle blue light awakens inside the machine. The nearby forms suggest workarounds, not a trashed or dysfunctional workplace. Screen has no readable text.

[3.00–5.00] Stretch time into controlled slow-motion separation. The computer housing lifts, then three internal trays separate vertically into an evenly spaced exploded diagram above the base. Monitor body and shell remain identifiable; parts stay rigid while traveling, without melting or fading. Inside, the trays reveal miniature physical representations of business processes rather than generic chips. The camera dives gently between the suspended layers. Strong parallax and moderate travel blur; brake smoothly into sharp macro holds. No violent debris or dizzying camera roll.

[5.00–8.00] First macro stop: three separate ivory customer-record cards each carry the same distinctive cobalt-blue circular token and the same non-readable ruled markings. The redundant cards slide physically into one aligned record holder, leaving one clear record. The blue token travels forward along a thin illuminated blue path. This action must visibly resolve duplicate information, not merely display attractive cards.

[8.00–11.00] Follow that same token to the next tray in one continuous move. A quote card waits before a visible gap. A simple blue check-mark approval tile seats onto the quote; only then does a connecting bridge align. The approved job card slides into a scheduling grid and connects to a tidy group of materials cubes. The office-to-work handoff visibly resumes after approval. Keep icons simple and nonverbal, with no readable text, statistics or floating dashboards.

[11.00–14.00] Bank gently to the last tray and settle into a close macro view. A job card gains a completion check, then its same blue token and ruled details carry forward into an invoice sheet and orderly reporting stack. Completion precedes invoice preparation. Do not show payment, currency, automatic collection, revenue increases or fabricated results. The continuous blue path now joins all three process trays.

[14.00–17.00] Swing out to frame the full exploded structure. The connected internal trays align; ivory and navy outer panels unfold along defined hinges into refined replacement surfaces while the internal identity remains intact. The transformation is an intentional mechanical reconfiguration from old shell to future workstation, not a dissolve. Components accelerate back along their ordered paths and seat with crisp stops, travel blur only during movement, outer housing closing last. Resolve into an elegant plausible navy future workstation: thin landscape screen, brushed metal stand, sculptural compact navy base and restrained blue seams. No gaming aesthetic or giant server. Arc toward a nearly frontal screen view. The screen illuminates to an evenly lit light ivory rectangle, EMPTY for exact official-logo compositing later; keep its four corners plainly visible and its interior free from glare or occluding objects.

[17.00–20.00] Lock the camera and workstation completely. No push, drift, screen animation or fade out. Hold the finished workstation and empty light ivory screen for three full seconds. Screen occupies enough of the frame for the later official logo to read. End on this still composition, fully framed, without a new scene. All captions and the official logo will be added separately; generate no text or logos anywhere in the film.
